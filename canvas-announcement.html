📊 How to Submit Your Scores

After completing all 5 games:

  1. Click the Export Data (CSV) button at the bottom
  2. A file will download to your computer
  3. Click this link to submit: Submit My Scores
  4. Upload the CSV file you just downloaded
  5. Done! You'll be on the leaderboard! 🎉

Don't worry - the file only contains your first name, age, grade, and scores. No personal info!
